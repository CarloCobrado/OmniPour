|Cashier|
start
:Log in;
:Select Customer Account;

|BEANTRACK System & Database|
:Auto-Apply Customer Price Tier
(SRP, Wholesale, Bulk, Dealer, Reseller);

|Cashier|
:Enter Products & Quantities;

|BEANTRACK System & Database|
:Validate & Calculate Order Total;

|Cashier|
:Select Payment Method
(Cash, Online, Terms);
:Submit & Complete Order;

|BEANTRACK System & Database|
:Save Sales Order Record;
if (Item Type?) then (Packaged Product)
  :Deduct Finished Goods Stock directly;
else (Recipe Beverage)
  :Retrieve Recipe BOM & Deduct
Raw Ingredients (g, ml, pcs);
endif
:Append SHA-256 Hash to Audit Ledger;

|Cashier|
:Print / Issue Order Receipt;
stop

|Inventory Staff|
start
:Log in;
if (Operational Task?) then (Inbound Delivery)
  :Receive Supplier Shipment
& Enter P.O., Cases, Units;
  |BEANTRACK System & Database|
  :Validate P.O. & Increment
Branch Inventory Stock;
else (Spoilage / Damage)
  |Inventory Staff|
  :Identify Damaged/Expired Stock
& Enter Mandatory Reason;
  |BEANTRACK System & Database|
  :Record Reason & Deduct
Spoiled Stock from Inventory;
endif

|BEANTRACK System & Database|
if (Stock <= Reorder Point?) then (Yes)
  :Flag Orange 'LOW' or
Red 'OUT OF STOCK' Alert;
else (No)
  :Maintain In-Stock Status;
endif

|Inventory Staff|
:View Updated Stock on Dashboard;
stop

|Supervisor|
start
:Log in;
:Configure Menu Drink Recipes
& Customer Pricing Tiers;
:Access Reporting Module
& Select Date Range;

|BEANTRACK System & Database|
:Retrieve Real-Time Sales, Deliveries,
Spoilage & Inventory Data;

|Supervisor|
if (Export Report?) then (Yes)
  |BEANTRACK System & Database|
  :Generate Verified Multi-Sheet
Client Excel Workbook (.xlsx);
  |Supervisor|
  :Download Client Report;
else (No)
  :Review On-Screen Summary;
endif
:Submit Report to Management;

|Management|
start
:Log in;
:Review Executive Dashboard
& Operational KPIs;
:Inspect Submitted Reports;
stop
